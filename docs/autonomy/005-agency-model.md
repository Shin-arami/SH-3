# 1. Purpose

This document defines the Agency Model architecture of the SH-3 Autonomy Layer.

The objectives are:

- Define autonomous agency
- Establish authority boundaries
- Manage autonomous responsibility
- Control self-directed operation
- Maintain governance and safety

---

# 2. Agency Philosophy

SH-3 Agency follows:

```text
Purpose

↓

Authority

↓

Responsibility

↓

Action

↓

Evaluation

↓

Improvement
```

Agency represents controlled autonomous capability.

---

# 3. Agency Definition

In SH-3, Agency means:

```text
The capability to independently manage goals,
make decisions, execute actions,
and improve behavior within defined boundaries.
```

Agency does not provide unlimited authority.

---
