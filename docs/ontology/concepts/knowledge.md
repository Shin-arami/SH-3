# SH-3 Concept: Knowledge

Version: 1.0.0
Status: Stable
Category: Ontology Concept
Semantic ID: SEM-CONCEPT-0007
Canonical Name: Knowledge
Author: SH-3 Architecture Working Group

---

# 1. Overview

Knowledge is the canonical semantic concept representing verified,
structured information that may be interpreted, reasoned over, shared,
and reused throughout the SH-3 AI Operating System Framework.

Knowledge is the primary semantic asset used by reasoning, planning,
learning, decision making, and autonomous execution.

Every intelligent behavior within SH-3 SHALL ultimately operate on
Knowledge.

---

# 2. Purpose

The Knowledge concept provides:

- Semantic understanding
- Decision support
- Reasoning input
- Learning foundation
- Planning information
- Federation exchange
- Explainability

Knowledge SHALL be implementation independent.

---

# 3. Canonical Definition

Knowledge is information whose semantic meaning has been defined within
the SH-3 Ontology and whose interpretation is governed by Context,
Relationships, and Policies.

Knowledge represents facts, assertions, models, or derived conclusions.

Knowledge SHALL NOT depend upon any specific storage technology.

---

# 4. Characteristics

Every Knowledge object SHALL be:

- Semantic
- Typed
- Traceable
- Explainable
- Versioned
- Context-aware
- Observable
- Machine readable

Knowledge MAY originate from observation, inference, learning, or
external sources.

# 7. Knowledge Categories

## Observational Knowledge

Knowledge derived directly from Events.

Examples:

- Runtime State
- Execution Result
- Sensor Observation

---

## Declarative Knowledge

Explicit semantic definitions.

Examples:

- Ontology Concepts
- Vocabulary
- Policies
- Specifications

---

## Procedural Knowledge

Knowledge describing execution methods.

Examples:

- Planning Strategy
- Optimization Rule
- Recovery Procedure

---

## Inferred Knowledge

Knowledge generated by reasoning.

Examples:

- Root Cause
- Capability Match
- Risk Assessment

---

## Learned Knowledge

Knowledge acquired through learning.

Examples:

- Behavioral Pattern
- Execution Heuristic
- Performance Model

---

## Federated Knowledge

Knowledge exchanged between SH-3 nodes.

Examples:

- Shared Ontology
- Shared Capability Metadata
- Distributed Knowledge Graph

# 9. Knowledge Lifecycle

Every Knowledge object SHALL progress through:

```
Observed

↓

Validated

↓

Stored

↓

Referenced

↓

Updated

↓

Archived
```

Knowledge SHALL preserve historical provenance throughout its lifecycle.

---

# 10. Knowledge Validation

Knowledge SHALL be validated for:

- Semantic correctness
- Context consistency
- Source integrity
- Relationship integrity
- Version compatibility
- Policy compliance

Invalid Knowledge SHALL NOT participate in reasoning.

---

# 11. Context Integration

Knowledge SHALL always be interpreted within Context.

Supported Contexts include:

- Runtime
- Mission
- Security
- Federation
- Environmental
- Temporal

Context SHALL qualify interpretation without altering semantic meaning.

---

# 12. Reasoning Integration

Reasoning engines SHALL consume Knowledge as their primary semantic
input.

Typical reasoning flow:

```
Knowledge

↓

Context Resolution

↓

Inference

↓

Decision

↓

Knowledge Update
```

Derived Knowledge SHALL retain provenance.

---

# 13. Learning Integration

Learning processes MAY:

- Generate Knowledge
- Refine Knowledge
- Merge Knowledge
- Classify Knowledge
- Retire obsolete Knowledge

Learning SHALL preserve semantic consistency.

---

# 14. Registry Integration

Knowledge SHALL reference:

- SEM Registry

Knowledge MAY additionally reference:

- CAP Registry
- EVT Registry
- MOD Registry
- XRF Registry

Registry identifiers SHALL remain synchronized.

---

# 15. Federation

Knowledge MAY be exchanged across federation members.

Federated exchange SHALL preserve:

- Semantic Identifier
- Context
- Provenance
- Confidence
- Version
- Relationships

Knowledge identity SHALL remain globally unique.

---

# 16. Constraints

Knowledge SHALL:

- Have one canonical semantic definition
- Preserve provenance
- Remain uniquely identifiable
- Support deterministic interpretation
- Be machine interpretable

Knowledge SHALL NOT contain implementation-specific semantics.

# 19. Summary

Knowledge is the canonical semantic concept representing verified,
structured information within the SH-3 AI Operating System Framework.

It serves as the foundation for reasoning, planning, learning,
decision-making, and federation by providing a stable,
implementation-independent representation of semantic information.

Knowledge enables explainable intelligence through deterministic
interpretation, traceable provenance, and standardized semantic
relationships.
