# SH-3 Concept: Federation

Version: 1.0.0
Status: Stable
Category: Ontology Concept
Semantic ID: SEM-CONCEPT-0014
Canonical Name: Federation
Author: SH-3 Architecture Working Group

---

# 1. Overview

Federation is the canonical semantic concept representing the
coordination, interoperability, and collective intelligence of multiple
independent SH-3 systems.

A Federation enables autonomous Kernels, Runtime environments, Services,
Agents, Knowledge repositories, and Missions to cooperate while
preserving semantic consistency and local autonomy.

Federation is the highest-level coordination concept within the SH-3 AI
Operating System Framework.

---

# 2. Purpose

The Federation concept provides:

- Distributed coordination
- Collective intelligence
- Knowledge sharing
- Mission collaboration
- Cross-node reasoning
- Resource federation
- Semantic interoperability
- Trust establishment

Federation enables multiple SH-3 systems to function as a unified
intelligent ecosystem.

---

# 3. Canonical Definition

A Federation is a semantically governed network of autonomous SH-3
systems that cooperate through standardized ontology, policies, events,
knowledge, and capabilities.

Each participating system SHALL preserve operational autonomy while
contributing to collective objectives.

---

# 4. Characteristics

Every Federation SHALL be:

- Distributed
- Autonomous
- Semantic
- Interoperable
- Policy-governed
- Observable
- Trust-aware
- Versioned

Federation SHALL preserve semantic compatibility across all members.

- Trust establishment
- Knowledge exchange

# 10. Trust Model

Federation SHALL define trust relationships among members.

Trust evaluation MAY consider:

- Identity verification
- Policy compliance
- Registry compatibility
- Reputation
- Provenance
- Security posture

Trust SHALL be continuously re-evaluated.

---

# 11. Knowledge Federation

Knowledge exchange SHALL preserve:

- Semantic Identifier
- Canonical meaning
- Provenance
- Confidence
- Relationships
- Version history

Knowledge conflicts SHOULD be resolved according to Federation Policy.

---

# 12. Mission Federation

Federation MAY support:

- Shared Missions
- Distributed Goals
- Cross-node Task allocation
- Collective execution
- Distributed monitoring

Mission ownership SHALL remain traceable.

---

# 13. Capability Federation

Federation SHALL enable:

- Capability discovery
- Capability advertisement
- Version negotiation
- Compatibility verification
- Cross-node invocation

Capability semantics SHALL remain identical across all members.

---

# 14. Registry Federation

Federation MAY synchronize:

- SEM Registry
- CAP Registry
- EVT Registry
- MOD Registry
- XRF Registry

Registry synchronization SHALL preserve semantic integrity and
identifier uniqueness.

---

# 15. Policy Federation

Federation SHALL support interoperable Policy evaluation.

Policies MAY define:

- Membership rules
- Trust requirements
- Data sharing permissions
- Security requirements
- Resource sharing limits

Policy conflicts SHALL be resolved through Federation Governance.

---

# 16. Observability

Every Federation SHALL expose:

- Member inventory
- Trust status
- Active Missions
- Shared Knowledge metrics
- Event throughput
- Registry synchronization status
- Health indicators

Observability SHALL support distributed diagnostics.

---

# 17. Constraints

A Federation SHALL:

- Preserve member autonomy
- Preserve semantic interoperability
- Preserve global identifier uniqueness
- Support deterministic synchronization
- Maintain complete provenance
- Enforce applicable Policies

Federation SHALL NOT redefine ontology semantics.

# 20. Summary

Federation is the canonical semantic concept representing distributed,
semantically interoperable collective intelligence within the SH-3 AI
Operating System Framework.

It provides the architectural foundation for cooperation among
autonomous SH-3 systems through standardized knowledge, capabilities,
events, policies, registries, and missions while preserving local
autonomy, deterministic behavior, and semantic consistency.

Federation completes the SH-3 Concept Layer by defining the highest level
of distributed intelligence and semantic coordination across the entire
SH-3 ecosystem.
