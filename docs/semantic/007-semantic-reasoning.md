Version:
1.0.0

Status:

---

# 1. Purpose

This document defines the Semantic Reasoning architecture of the SH-3 Semantic Layer.

The objectives are:

- Enable meaning-based inference
- Derive knowledge from relationships
- Support intelligent decision making
- Connect knowledge with cognition
- Provide explainable reasoning results

---

# 2. Semantic Reasoning Philosophy

SH-3 Semantic Reasoning follows:

```
Knowledge

↓

Relationship

↓

Context

↓

Inference

↓

Understanding

↓

Decision Support
```

Reasoning derives meaning from structured semantic knowledge.
