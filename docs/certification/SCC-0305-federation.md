# SCC-0305
# SH-3 Federation Certification Specification

Version: 1.0
Status: Stable
Category: Certification Specification
Author: SH-3 Architecture Working Group

---

# 1. Overview

This specification defines the Federation Certification requirements
for the SH-3 AI Operating System Framework.

Federation Certification verifies that an implementation can securely,
reliably, and deterministically participate in SH-3 federations across
multiple organizations, infrastructures, geographic regions, and
administrative domains.

This certification represents the highest certification level within
the SH-3 certification framework.

---

# 2. Objectives

Federation Certification SHALL verify:

- Federation interoperability
- Federation discovery
- Distributed coordination
- Distributed mission execution
- Distributed knowledge exchange
- Distributed semantic intelligence
- Federation governance
- Universal interoperability

---

# 3. Certification Scope

Certification SHALL evaluate:

- Federation Protocol
- Knowledge Exchange
- Semantic Exchange
- Mission Coordination
- Governance Coordination
- Trust Management
- Cross-Domain Operation
- Collective Intelligence

---

RFC-0265 Federation Architecture

RFC-0266 Collective Intelligence Architecture

RFC-0268 Distributed Knowledge Evolution

RFC-0269 Semantic Intelligence Layer

RFC-0270 Self Evolution

# 5. Federation Discovery

Certification SHALL verify:

- Node Discovery
- Service Discovery
- Capability Discovery
- Semantic Discovery
- Trust Discovery
- Federation Registration

Discovery SHALL remain deterministic.

---

# 6. Federation Identity

Certification SHALL verify:

- Global Node Identity
- Organization Identity
- Cluster Identity
- Service Identity
- Agent Identity
- Certificate Validation

Federation identities SHALL be globally unique.

---

# 7. Trust Verification

Certification SHALL verify:

- Identity Trust
- Capability Trust
- Semantic Trust
- Knowledge Trust
- Governance Trust
- Historical Reputation

Trust SHALL be continuously evaluated.

---

# 8. Distributed Mission Execution

Certification SHALL verify:

- Mission Delegation
- Mission Coordination
- Mission Monitoring
- Mission Recovery
- Mission Completion
- Cross-Domain Execution

Mission execution SHALL remain deterministic.

---

# 9. Knowledge Federation

Certification SHALL verify:

- Knowledge Publication
- Knowledge Discovery
- Knowledge Synchronization
- Provenance Preservation
- Conflict Resolution
- Distributed Consistency

Knowledge SHALL remain semantically consistent.

---

# 10. Semantic Federation

Certification SHALL verify:

- Ontology Compatibility
- Concept Mapping
- Semantic Negotiation
- Context Sharing
- Intent Exchange
- Capability Understanding

Semantic interoperability SHALL remain platform independent.

---

# 11. Governance Federation

Certification SHALL verify:

- Policy Distribution
- Trust Coordination
- Compliance Verification
- Audit Synchronization
- Governance Negotiation
- Distributed Decision Traceability

Governance SHALL remain consistent across federation domains.

---

# 12. Collective Intelligence

Certification SHALL verify:

- Knowledge Aggregation
- Distributed Reasoning
- Collaborative Planning
- Consensus Formation
- Shared Learning
- Collective Optimization

Collective intelligence SHALL preserve individual autonomy.

---

# 13. Distributed Intelligence

Certification SHALL verify:

- Distributed Planning
- Distributed Learning
- Distributed Optimization
- Distributed Decision Making
- Distributed Memory
- Distributed Semantic Reasoning

Distributed intelligence SHALL remain reproducible.

---

# 14. Security

Certification SHALL verify:

- Mutual TLS
- Identity Authentication
- Authorization
- Digital Signatures
- Secure Federation Channels
- Audit Logging

Federation communications SHALL be cryptographically protected.

---

# 15. Reliability

Certification SHALL verify:

- Node Recovery
- Network Recovery
- Federation Reconfiguration
- Service Failover
- State Synchronization
- Fault Isolation

Federation SHALL remain resilient.

---

# 16. Scalability

Certification SHALL verify:

- Horizontal Scaling
- Cluster Expansion
- Multi-Region Operation
- Cross-Cloud Operation
- Federation Growth

Scalability SHALL preserve protocol compatibility.

---

# 17. Performance

Certification SHALL verify:

- Discovery Latency
- Synchronization Latency
- Federation Throughput
- Knowledge Propagation
- Semantic Resolution
- Mission Coordination

Performance SHALL be measurable.

---

# 18. Certification Evidence

Certification SHALL produce:

- Federation Compliance Report
- Trust Verification Report
- Mission Coordination Report
- Knowledge Federation Report
- Semantic Federation Report
- Governance Federation Report
- Performance Report

Evidence SHALL be machine readable.

---

# 19. Certification Decision

Federation Certification SHALL result in:

- Federation Certified
- Federation Certified with Observations
- Federation Certification Deferred
- Federation Certification Denied

Every decision SHALL include objective supporting evidence.

---

# 20. Relationship to Other Certifications

Federation Certification SHALL complement:

- SCC-0301 Conformance Certification
- SCC-0302 Architecture Certification
- SCC-0303 Protocol Certification
- SCC-0304 Semantic Certification

Federation Certification SHALL represent the highest level of SH-3
implementation certification.

---

# 21. Certification Levels

Federation Certification MAY be granted for:

- Local Federation
- Enterprise Federation
- Regional Federation
- Global Federation
- Universal Federation

Certification SHALL specify the highest validated federation scope.

---

# 22. Future Compatibility

Certification SHALL support:

- Future Federation Architectures
- Future Intelligence Models
- Future Semantic Extensions
- Future Governance Models
- Future Distributed Computing Platforms

Evolution SHALL preserve interoperability.

---

# 23. Summary

The SH-3 Federation Certification Specification defines the highest
certification standard within the SH-3 AI Operating System Framework.
It validates that independently implemented SH-3 systems can securely,
deterministically, and semantically interoperate across organizations,
clouds, clusters, and global federations while preserving governance,
trust, collective intelligence, and universal interoperability.
