# SPP-0105
# SH-3 Governance Protocol Specification

Version: 1.0
Status: Stable
Category: Protocol Specification
Author: SH-3 Architecture Working Group

---

# 1. Overview

This specification defines the SH-3 Governance Protocol (SGP).

The Governance Protocol establishes the standard framework for policy
management, trust evaluation, compliance verification, decision
traceability, and autonomous governance throughout the SH-3 AI
Operating System Framework.

The protocol enables every SH-3 implementation to operate under
consistent governance while preserving local autonomy and federation
interoperability.

---

# 2. Objectives

The protocol SHALL provide:

- Distributed governance
- Policy interoperability
- Deterministic policy evaluation
- Trust management
- Compliance verification
- Decision accountability
- Federation governance
- Autonomous governance

---

# 3. Design Principles

The protocol SHALL follow:

- Governance First
- Everything Event Driven
- Deterministic Processing
- Policy Driven
- Trust by Verification
- Explainable Decisions
- Immutable Governance Events

---

# 4. Governance Model

Governance SHALL consist of:

- Policies
- Rules
- Constraints
- Permissions
- Obligations
- Risks
- Compliance Requirements
- Audit Records

Every governance object SHALL possess a globally unique identifier.

---

# 5. Governance Scope

Governance MAY apply to:

- Nodes
- Agents
- Services
- Plugins
- Workflows
- Missions
- Knowledge
- Semantic Objects
- Federation Domains

Governance SHALL be hierarchical.

---

# 6. Policy Model

Every policy SHALL define:

- Policy ID
- Name
- Version
- Scope
- Priority
- Evaluation Rules
- Actions
- Exceptions
- Metadata

Policies SHALL be immutable after publication.

---

# 7. Policy Lifecycle

Policies SHALL progress through:

Draft

Validated

Approved

Published

Activated

Updated

Deprecated

Archived

Lifecycle transitions SHALL be auditable.

---

# 8. Policy Distribution

The protocol SHALL support:

- Local Distribution
- Cluster Distribution
- Federation Distribution
- Incremental Updates
- Scheduled Distribution
- Event-Driven Distribution

Policy distribution SHALL be authenticated.

---

# 9. Policy Evaluation

Evaluation SHALL support:

- Pre-Execution Evaluation
- Runtime Evaluation
- Post-Execution Evaluation
- Continuous Evaluation
- Context-Aware Evaluation
- Semantic Evaluation

Evaluation SHALL remain deterministic.

---

# 10. Trust Management

Trust SHALL include:

- Identity Verification
- Capability Verification
- Reputation
- Historical Performance
- Policy Compliance
- Security Assessment
- Federation Trust

Trust SHALL be continuously updated.

---

# 11. Compliance Verification

Compliance SHALL verify:

- Architecture Compliance
- Protocol Compliance
- Semantic Compliance
- Policy Compliance
- Mission Compliance
- Security Compliance
- Federation Compliance

Compliance SHALL generate verifiable evidence.

---

# 12. Decision Provenance

Every governance decision SHALL record:

- Decision ID
- Decision Context
- Input Evidence
- Policies Applied
- Responsible Entity
- Timestamp
- Outcome
- Confidence

Decision provenance SHALL never be discarded.

---

# 13. Audit Protocol

Audit SHALL support:

- Continuous Audit
- Event Audit
- Policy Audit
- Mission Audit
- Knowledge Audit
- Security Audit
- Federation Audit

Audit records SHALL be immutable.

---

# 14. Governance Events

Every governance operation SHALL generate immutable events.

Standard events include:

- PolicyCreated
- PolicyValidated
- PolicyActivated
- PolicyEvaluated
- PolicyViolated
- TrustUpdated
- ComplianceVerified
- ComplianceFailed
- AuditGenerated
- GovernanceDecision

Events SHALL be replayable.

---

# 15. Federation Governance

The protocol SHALL support:

- Cross-Domain Policies
- Federation Trust
- Shared Governance
- Delegated Governance
- Governance Synchronization
- Governance Negotiation

Federation governance SHALL preserve domain sovereignty.

---

# 16. Autonomous Governance

The implementation SHALL support:

- Automatic Policy Evaluation
- Automatic Compliance Verification
- Automatic Trust Updates
- Automatic Risk Detection
- Automatic Escalation
- Human Approval Integration

Autonomous actions SHALL remain auditable.

---

# 17. Security

Governance SHALL support:

- Mutual TLS
- Digital Signature
- Policy Integrity Verification
- Encryption
- Identity Authentication
- Authorization
- Secure Audit Storage

Governance artifacts SHALL be cryptographically verifiable.

---

# 18. Compatibility

The protocol SHALL remain compatible with:

- SPS-0001 Embedded Profile
- SPS-0002 Edge Profile
- SPS-0003 Server Profile
- SPS-0004 Cluster Profile
- SPS-0005 Global Federation Profile

Governance interoperability SHALL be profile independent.

---

# 19. Extensibility

The protocol SHALL support:

- Domain Policies
- Industry Profiles
- Vendor Extensions
- Regional Regulations
- Research Policies
- Future SH-3 Governance Extensions

Unknown extensions SHALL NOT invalidate protocol processing.

---

# 20. Compliance

A compliant implementation SHALL:

- Support policy lifecycle management
- Support deterministic policy evaluation
- Support trust management
- Support compliance verification
- Support immutable governance events
- Support audit generation
- Pass SH-3 protocol certification

---

# 21. Relationship to Other Specifications

This protocol integrates with:

- RFC-0264 Meta Governance Architecture
- SPP-0101 Federation Protocol
- SPP-0102 Knowledge Exchange Protocol
- SPP-0103 Semantic Exchange Protocol
- SPP-0104 Mission Protocol

Governance SHALL apply consistently across all SH-3 protocols.

---

# 22. Summary

The SH-3 Governance Protocol defines the governance foundation of the
SH-3 AI Operating System Framework. It standardizes policy management,
trust evaluation, compliance verification, decision provenance, audit,
and federation governance, enabling autonomous systems to operate in a
secure, explainable, deterministic, and interoperable manner across the
entire SH-3 ecosystem.
