# Abstract

This RFC defines the SH-3 Self Evolution Architecture (SEA).

Self Evolution is the highest-level adaptive capability that enables SH-3 to continuously improve itself while preserving architectural integrity, semantic consistency, governance constraints, and operational stability.

Unlike isolated learning mechanisms, Self Evolution governs the coordinated evolution of knowledge, reasoning, planning, architecture, policies, workflows, and intelligence capabilities.

All evolution SHALL remain observable, explainable, reversible, and governed.

This document is normative.

# Core Principles

## Principle 1

Evolution SHALL Preserve Identity.

Core architectural identity SHALL remain stable across evolution.

---

## Principle 2

Evolution SHALL Be Governed.

Every evolution SHALL comply with Meta Governance policies.

---

## Principle 3

Evolution SHALL Be Explainable.

Every change SHALL include rationale, evidence, and expected impact.

---

## Principle 4

Evolution SHALL Be Reversible.

Approved evolution SHALL support rollback.

---

## Principle 5

Evolution SHALL Improve Utility.

Evolution SHALL demonstrate measurable benefit before becoming canonical.

# Simulation Requirements

Evolution SHALL be evaluated through one or more of:

- sandbox execution
- historical replay
- scenario simulation
- policy verification
- formal validation

Direct production deployment without validation SHALL NOT occur.
