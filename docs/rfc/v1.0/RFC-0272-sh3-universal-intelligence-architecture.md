# Abstract

This RFC defines the SH-3 Universal Intelligence Architecture (UIA).

Universal Intelligence is the highest architectural layer of SH-3. It unifies all architectural domains—including execution, cognition, semantics, governance, evolution, federation, and intelligence—under a single coherent operating model.

Universal Intelligence does not replace specialized intelligence domains. Instead, it provides the principles, interfaces, and coordination mechanisms that allow them to operate as one adaptive system.

This document is normative.

# Core Principles

## Principle 1

Everything SHALL Be Integrated.

Every architectural layer SHALL participate in a unified operating model.

---

## Principle 2

Everything SHALL Be Governed.

All behavior SHALL comply with constitutional and governance constraints.

---

## Principle 3

Everything SHALL Be Explainable.

Every significant decision, adaptation, and evolution SHALL be traceable.

---

## Principle 4

Everything SHALL Be Replaceable.

All components SHALL expose stable interfaces that permit replacement without breaking architectural contracts.

---

## Principle 5

Everything SHALL Continuously Evolve.

The system SHALL support controlled improvement across all layers.
