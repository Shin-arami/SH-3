# Abstract

This RFC defines the SH-3 General Intelligence Architecture (GIA).

General Intelligence is the unified cognitive architecture that integrates all intelligence capabilities defined by SH-3 into a coherent, adaptive, explainable, and governable system.

Rather than representing a single reasoning engine, General Intelligence coordinates multiple specialized intelligence domains—including semantic, architectural, autonomous, collective, and executive intelligence—through a common operating model.

The objective is to enable SH-3 to pursue arbitrary goals across diverse environments while preserving architectural integrity, governance, and semantic consistency.

This document is normative.

# Core Principles

## Principle 1

Intelligence SHALL Be Unified.

All intelligence domains SHALL cooperate through a common model.

---

## Principle 2

Intelligence SHALL Be Modular.

Every intelligence capability SHALL remain independently replaceable.

---

## Principle 3

Intelligence SHALL Be Explainable.

Reasoning SHALL preserve complete evidence chains.

---

## Principle 4

Intelligence SHALL Be Governed.

All intelligence SHALL operate under Meta Governance.

---

## Principle 5

Intelligence SHALL Continuously Improve.

General Intelligence SHALL integrate validated improvements through Self Evolution.
