# Abstract

This RFC defines the SH-3 Collective Intelligence Architecture (CIA).

Collective Intelligence enables multiple SH-3 intelligence entities to combine knowledge, reasoning capabilities, experiences, and decision processes to produce emergent intelligence beyond the capability of individual nodes.

The architecture defines mechanisms for:

- shared intelligence
- distributed reasoning
- collective memory
- collaborative decision making
- knowledge synthesis
- consensus formation

Collective Intelligence SHALL preserve individual autonomy while enabling cooperative intelligence amplification.

This document is normative.

# Core Principles

## Principle 1

Collective Intelligence SHALL Preserve Individual Intelligence.

Each node SHALL maintain independent identity and autonomy.

---

## Principle 2

Collective Intelligence SHALL Require Contribution.

Shared intelligence SHALL originate from validated contributions.

---

## Principle 3

Collective Intelligence SHALL Be Explainable.

Collective decisions SHALL preserve reasoning traces.

---

## Principle 4

Collective Intelligence SHALL Be Governed.

Collective actions SHALL follow governance constraints.

---

## Principle 5

Collective Intelligence SHALL Improve Over Time.

The collective system SHALL support continuous learning.
