# Abstract

This RFC defines the SH-3 Distributed Knowledge Evolution Architecture (DKEA).

The architecture enables geographically and logically distributed SH-3 nodes to continuously improve shared intelligence through the controlled evolution of knowledge rather than simple model synchronization.

Knowledge Evolution encompasses:

- knowledge acquisition
- knowledge validation
- experience integration
- reasoning refinement
- policy improvement
- workflow optimization
- architectural knowledge evolution

The architecture preserves node autonomy while enabling continuous collective improvement.

This document is normative.

# Evolution Principles

## Principle 1

Knowledge Evolves Faster Than Software.

Knowledge SHALL evolve independently from executable implementation.

---

## Principle 2

Experience Becomes Knowledge.

Validated operational experience SHALL be eligible for knowledge integration.

---

## Principle 3

Knowledge Requires Validation.

Unverified knowledge SHALL never become canonical.

---

## Principle 4

Knowledge SHALL Preserve Provenance.

Every knowledge element SHALL retain:

- origin
- author
- confidence
- validation history
- evolution lineage

---

## Principle 5

Evolution SHALL Be Reversible.

Every accepted knowledge revision SHALL support rollback.
