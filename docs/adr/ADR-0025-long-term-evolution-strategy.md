# ADR-0025: Long-Term Evolution Strategy

Version: 2.0
Status: Accepted
Category: Architecture Decision Record
Author: SH-3 Architecture Working Group

---

# 1. Context

SH-3 is designed as an AI Operating System intended to evolve over
multiple generations of architecture, implementation, deployment, and
intelligence.

Technologies, execution environments, hardware platforms, protocols,
reasoning models, and operational requirements will continuously
change.

The architecture SHALL therefore define a long-term strategy that
preserves architectural integrity while enabling continuous evolution.

---

# 2. Decision

SH-3 adopts the architectural principle:

> **Architectural evolution SHALL be continuous, deterministic,
> compatible, and governed.**

Long-term evolution SHALL prioritize architectural stability over
short-term optimization.

---

# 3. Motivation

A long-term evolution strategy provides:

- Sustainable architecture
- Predictable modernization
- Controlled innovation
- Long-term interoperability
- Stable ecosystem growth
- Continuous operational reliability

Without a long-term strategy:

- Architecture becomes fragmented.
- Specifications diverge.
- Compatibility deteriorates.
- Technical debt accumulates.
- Governance weakens over time.

---

# 4. Evolution Objectives

Long-term evolution SHALL support:

```text
Architecture Improvement

Capability Expansion

Knowledge Growth

Operational Optimization

Federation Expansion

Technology Adoption
```

Evolution SHALL preserve architectural consistency.

---

# 5. Evolution Principles

Every architectural change SHALL satisfy:

```text
Deterministic

Observable

Traceable

Validated

Governed

Reversible
```

Evolution SHALL never compromise system integrity.

---

# 6. Evolution Domains

Long-term evolution MAY affect:

```text
Architecture

Runtime

Kernel

Infrastructure

Knowledge

Ontology

Security

Federation

Deployment

Developer Tooling
```

Each domain SHALL evolve independently where practical.

---

# 7. Compatibility Strategy

Evolution SHALL preserve:

```text
Public Interfaces

Protocols

Schemas

Events

Configuration

Deployment Artifacts
```

Breaking changes SHALL remain exceptional.

---

# 8. Knowledge Preservation

Knowledge SHALL remain durable across versions.

Preservation SHALL include:

```text
Ontology

Knowledge Graphs

Policies

Memory

Learning Results

Operational History
```

Knowledge migration SHALL be deterministic.

---

# 9. Federation Evolution

Federated SH-3 systems SHALL support:

```text
Incremental Upgrades

Mixed-Version Operation

Protocol Negotiation

Capability Discovery

Compatibility Validation
```

Federation SHALL remain operational throughout upgrades.

---

# 10. Registry Evolution

The Specification Registry SHALL maintain:

```text
Version History

Migration Records

Compatibility Matrix

Deprecation History

Architecture Relationships
```

Historical records SHALL remain immutable.

---

# 11. Governance

Architectural evolution SHALL require:

```text
Proposal

Review

Impact Analysis

Risk Assessment

Approval

Validation

Publication
```

Governance SHALL remain transparent and reproducible.

---

# 12. Certification

Every architectural evolution SHALL trigger:

```text
Conformance Validation

Security Validation

Performance Validation

Semantic Validation

Federation Validation
```

Certification SHALL remain continuous.

---

# 13. Technical Debt Management

Technical debt SHALL be actively managed.

Management SHALL include:

```text
Identification

Classification

Prioritization

Mitigation

Retirement
```

Architectural debt SHALL be minimized through continuous improvement.

---

# 14. Observability

Evolution SHALL remain measurable through:

```text
Architecture Metrics

Performance Metrics

Reliability Metrics

Compatibility Metrics

Adoption Metrics
```

Metrics SHALL guide future improvements.

---

# 15. Automation

Long-term evolution SHOULD maximize automation for:

```text
Architecture Validation

Dependency Analysis

Compatibility Verification

Specification Generation

Impact Analysis

Certification
```

Automation SHALL improve consistency and repeatability.

---

# 16. Continuous Improvement

Continuous improvement SHALL operate through:

```text
Operational Feedback

Learning

Analysis

Optimization

Governance

Evolution
```

Improvement SHALL remain evidence-driven.

---

# 17. Success Criteria

Long-term evolution SHALL maintain:

```text
Architecture Stability

Operational Reliability

Semantic Consistency

Knowledge Integrity

Federation Compatibility

Developer Productivity
```

Success SHALL be continuously evaluated.

---

# 18. Benefits

The long-term evolution strategy provides:

- Sustainable architecture
- Predictable modernization
- Reduced technical debt
- Long-term interoperability
- Controlled innovation
- Reliable autonomous evolution

---

# 19. Consequences

Advantages

- Stable architectural foundation
- Controlled ecosystem growth
- Simplified modernization
- Strong governance
- Reliable certification
- Long-term maintainability

Trade-offs

- Increased governance effort
- More comprehensive validation
- Larger specification ecosystem
- Greater architectural discipline

These trade-offs are accepted to ensure that SH-3 remains a sustainable
AI Operating System over multiple generations.
